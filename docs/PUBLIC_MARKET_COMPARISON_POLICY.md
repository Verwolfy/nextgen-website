# Product information: publication rules
- This directory contains **market comparison** data from external manufacturers, not approved FENEK laboratory measurements.
- None of the fields `observed`, `source_ids`, or suggested conditioning may be described as a NextGen composition, mixing dose, consumption, open time, SDS or certified class.
- Source G19 excluded due to an implausible unverified unit (`7 to 10 L/m²`).
- Source values are brief summaries. They are market-specific and require checking the linked current manufacturer datasheet.
- Product names that resemble standardized classifications are not proof of conformity.
- FENEK product data must be released only after formula, test report, FDS and approvals are provided by NextGen.
- Site pages are **informational and comparatives**; they do not contain a real construction application instruction.
